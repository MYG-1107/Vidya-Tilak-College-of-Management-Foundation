Eligibility

Understand the typical academic entry point before you apply.

These are guidance-level patterns only. The university or institution’s current programme notice is the final source for eligibility.

StageTypical entry qualificationCommon examplesWhat to verify
Undergraduate10+2 / Intermediate or equivalentBA, BBA, B.Com, BCAMinimum marks, subject requirements, age/entrance rules
PostgraduateBachelor’s degreeMBA, MCA, M.Com, MA, M.Sc.Discipline, minimum marks, entrance/work experience
PhDMaster’s / qualifying degree as specifiedPhD in management, education, science, humanities etc.Research area, entrance test, proposal/interview, supervisor availability
Post-DoctoralCompleted PhD or equivalent research qualificationPost-doctoral fellowship / research projectHost institution, research theme, funding, duration and eligibility

Why exact verification matters

For online and distance programmes, the permitted mode and entitlement/recognition status can be session-specific. For regular and research admissions, institutional rules can differ by school, discipline and intake.

Online / ODL verification: Recognition can be session- and programme-specific. Confirm the relevant institution and programme on the current UGC DEB information before admission.
