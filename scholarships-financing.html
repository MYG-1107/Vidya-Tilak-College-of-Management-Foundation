Affordability & planning

Scholarships, Fee Planning & Education Finance Guide

Understand the questions to ask about scholarships, concessions, instalments, loans and the total cost of a programme before you apply.

Plan the full cost

Look beyond the advertised tuition fee.

Universities may publish scholarships, concessions, financing or instalment options. Availability and eligibility are institution-specific, so verify the current session directly.

Scholarships

Ask who is eligible

Common categories may include merit, defence personnel, alumni, disability-related concessions or regional categories, depending on the institution. Ask for the current written policy.

Payment plans

Compare semester, annual and full-payment options

Check whether payment frequency changes the total payable amount, and ask about application fees, examination fees and non-refundable charges.

Loans & EMI

Understand financing terms

If an institution advertises financing or no-cost EMI, verify the lender, tenure, processing charges, eligibility and whether scholarships can be combined with the financing.

Total cost

Build your personal budget

Include tuition, admission fees, examinations, travel or campus visits, devices, internet, books, certifications and opportunity cost where relevant.

Important:

Fee figures and scholarship offers change by programme, session, student category and payment plan. Vidya Tilak does not guarantee a scholarship, discount, loan approval or fixed fee. Verify the current official university notice before paying.
