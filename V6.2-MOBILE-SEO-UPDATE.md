Vidya Tilak College — v6.2 Mobile + SEO Update

Mobile-only changes:
- Better hamburger button with a cleaner open/close state.
- Mobile navigation uses a compact two-column layout with full-width priority actions.
- Mobile navigation remains independently scrollable and reserves space for the fixed Call/Enquire bar.
- Mobile footer is shorter: brand copy/badges are condensed, link groups use two columns, partner note is hidden, and contact text is tightened.
- Desktop CSS is unchanged by the v6.2 patch because the layout overrides are inside mobile media queries.

SEO audit and updates:
- 56 indexable HTML pages checked (includes/header/footer fragments excluded).
- All 56 pages have unique <title>, meta description, canonical, robots and at least one H1.
- All 56 pages now have a page-specific meta keywords tag for requested completeness.
- 14 previously short meta descriptions were expanded and synchronized with Open Graph/Twitter descriptions.
- 6 pages that lacked JSON-LD now include EducationalOrganization structured data.
- Sitemap and robots.txt were already present and retained.

Important: Google states that the meta-keywords tag is not used for Google Search ranking. Ranking-relevant work remains the unique titles/descriptions, useful page content, internal linking, crawlability, and valid structured data.

The package intentionally excludes logos and other binary assets so your current logos/ folder is preserved.
