Business management programmes introduce how organisations set goals, serve customers, manage people, allocate resources and improve operations. A specialisation adds depth in an area, but its name is only a starting point. Students should compare subjects, projects, faculty expertise and the work they want to understand.

## Common areas of specialisation

Marketing focuses on customer needs, positioning, communication, research and channels. Finance studies how organisations manage funds, analyse performance, evaluate investments and understand risk. Human resources explores recruitment, learning, performance, employee relations and organisational development. Operations and supply chain cover process design, sourcing, logistics, quality and delivery.

Business analytics uses data and quantitative methods to support decisions. Entrepreneurship focuses on opportunity discovery, business models, resource constraints and venture development. Some programmes combine areas, such as marketing analytics, healthcare management or fintech.

## Compare curriculum and assessment

Look beyond the specialisation title. Review course descriptions, case studies, data tools, group assignments, internships, simulations and capstone projects. Ask whether the programme helps students apply concepts to a realistic organisational problem. A practical project should explain the question, evidence, recommendation and limitations.

## Match the subject with your experience and goals

A learner with a commerce background may be interested in finance or analytics, while someone who enjoys customer research may prefer marketing. A person with logistics experience may value operations or supply chain. Eligibility varies, and some programmes may require specific prior qualifications.

## Questions to ask before admission

- What are the core and elective subjects?
- Which specialisations are available in the current intake?
- Are internships, live projects or case studies included?
- What quantitative or technical prerequisites apply?
- What is the complete fee schedule and refund policy?
- Which institution awards the qualification?

## Choose based on learning, not a label

Compare current syllabi and ask how students are assessed. Review [PG programme pathways](/pg-programmes.html) and the [programme catalogue](/program-catalogue.html), then confirm availability and eligibility directly with the institution.

*Specialisation titles, admission criteria and outcomes vary by institution. Employment is not guaranteed by a qualification alone.*
