Commerce students may consider several finance-related directions, but the right route depends on interests, strengths, qualifications and role requirements. Finance is a broad field that includes accounting, banking, corporate finance, financial operations, risk, analysis and financial technology.

## Explore the main areas

Accounting focuses on recording, reporting and interpreting financial information. Banking includes customer services, lending, operations, compliance and risk. Corporate finance supports budgeting, funding, financial planning and performance analysis within an organisation. Investment-related roles may involve research, portfolio analysis or client support and can carry specific regulatory requirements.

Risk and compliance work looks at how organisations identify, monitor and control financial and operational exposures. Financial analysis uses data, accounting knowledge and business context to explain performance and support decisions.

## Skills that strengthen a finance profile

Students can build accounting fundamentals, spreadsheet skills, quantitative reasoning, business writing, ethics and attention to detail. Data tools and financial modelling may be useful for specific roles. Learn to trace a number to its source, document assumptions and communicate uncertainty instead of presenting estimates as facts.

## Compare qualifications and professional routes

Some roles require a relevant degree, professional examination, registration or supervised experience. Requirements differ across employers and regulators. Before paying for a programme, check whether it is academic education, professional exam preparation or a separate credential. Confirm the organisation that issues any certificate and what recognition it has.

## Questions to ask before choosing

- Does the course cover accounting, economics, finance and quantitative methods?
- Are financial statements and spreadsheets used in practical assignments?
- Does it include risk, compliance, ethics and data interpretation?
- Are professional examination fees separate from tuition?
- What eligibility or experience is required for target roles?

## Plan a gradual route

Start with a strong commerce or business foundation. Build a small portfolio such as a budget analysis, financial statement review or practice model using public data. Explore [business and finance](/business-finance-leadership.html) and the [programme catalogue](/program-catalogue.html).

*This is a study and career overview, not financial or investment advice. A qualification does not guarantee a role or authorisation.*
