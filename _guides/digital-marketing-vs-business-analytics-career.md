Digital marketing and business analytics both use data, but they start with different questions. Digital marketing asks how to reach, inform and convert an intended audience through digital channels. Business analytics asks what available data says about organisational performance and what decisions it may support. The fields overlap through measurement, experimentation and customer insights.

## What digital marketing focuses on

Digital marketing may include search visibility, content, social media, email, paid campaigns, landing pages and conversion measurement. Students need to understand audience research, positioning, content quality, channel selection and the customer journey. Good practice is not simply publishing more posts; it means setting a clear goal and evaluating whether activity helps users.

## What business analytics focuses on

Business analytics works with data from operations, sales, finance, product usage or customer interactions. Common foundations include statistics, spreadsheets, SQL, data cleaning, visualisation and communication. Analysts should understand how data was collected, what is missing and which conclusions are justified. A dashboard is useful only when its measures are defined and its users understand the limitations.

## Where the paths meet

Marketing analytics, customer segmentation, campaign attribution and conversion-rate analysis bring the disciplines together. A digital marketer may use dashboards to evaluate channel performance, while an analyst may investigate business factors behind changes in customer behaviour. Both require clear questions and careful interpretation.

## Choose based on the work you enjoy

- Prefer writing, audience research and channel experiments? Explore digital marketing.
- Prefer structured data, statistics and decision support? Explore business analytics.
- Interested in both? Build foundations in marketing principles and data analysis before choosing a specialisation.
