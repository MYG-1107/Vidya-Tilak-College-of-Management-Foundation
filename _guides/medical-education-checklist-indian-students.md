Choosing a medical programme is a high-stakes decision for students and families. A low headline fee or attractive campus photograph is not enough to judge whether a course is suitable. The programme, institution, clinical learning arrangements, costs and professional recognition all need careful verification before any payment.

## Confirm the institution and exact programme

Identify the legal name of the awarding institution, campus, programme title, study mode and admission intake. Check current status on the official regulator or government source relevant to the country and qualification. If studying outside India, confirm current rules that apply to Indian students and future registration in India. Requirements can change, so use current official notices rather than old forum posts.

## Examine the learning and clinical environment

Ask where teaching takes place, how clinical exposure is arranged, what languages are used in instruction and patient settings, and what supervised practical experience is included. Request written details about curriculum, assessment, attendance, internship or clinical rotations and student support. Confirm that the advertised facilities are actually accessible to students in the programme.

## Calculate the full cost

Request a written breakdown covering tuition, registration, examination, laboratory, accommodation, travel, insurance, visa, food and other compulsory charges. Ask when payments are due, which amounts are refundable and who receives the money. Avoid payments to personal accounts or relying on verbal promises.

## A medical education due-diligence checklist

- Verify the institution and exact programme from current official sources.
- Confirm relevant admission tests and eligibility rules.
- Check curriculum, clinical training, internship and language requirements.
- Obtain the full fee schedule and written refund terms.
- Confirm visa, accommodation and student-support arrangements.
- Keep copies of official offers, receipts and correspondence.
- Seek independent advice before signing documents or transferring funds.

## Make a decision only after verification

Review the [MBBS abroad guidance page](/mbbs-abroad.html), [eligibility guide](/eligibility.html) and [documents checklist](/documentation.html). Vidya Tilak can help structure questions, but the responsible university and regulators determine admission and professional recognition.
