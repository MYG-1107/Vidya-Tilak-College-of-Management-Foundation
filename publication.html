Research • Publication Support

Publication Support for Researchers & Academic Scholars

Publication can be the final stage of months of research. Vidya Tilak provides academic publication support covering manuscript refinement, journal-fit review, formatting, submission readiness and post-submission response support.

Discuss Your ManuscriptExplore Journal Resources
End-to-end support

From manuscript preparation to journal submission.

Support can be tailored to the stage your paper is at. The aim is to improve clarity, presentation, consistency and compliance with the target journal's instructions—not to guarantee acceptance.

01

Journal fit & selection

Assess the subject area, manuscript scope, article type and author guidelines before selecting a target journal.

02

English editing & polishing

Improve language clarity, academic tone, consistency, grammar and presentation while retaining the author's research meaning.

03

Technical review

Check structure, tables, figures, references, formatting and other manuscript presentation requirements.

04

Journal formatting

Adapt the manuscript to the current author guidelines of the chosen journal, including citation and submission formatting.

05

Submission readiness

Prepare the manuscript package, cover-letter inputs and submission checklist for the journal's official workflow.

06

Reviewer-response support

Help structure responses to reviewer comments and identify revisions that should be addressed in the manuscript.

07

Plagiarism-check guidance

Review originality and similarity concerns and support ethical revision practices before submission.

08

Custom support packages

Choose editing, formatting, journal-fit review, submission preparation or a broader combination based on your research stage.

Advantages

Why researchers use publication assistance.

Expert contact

Keep your time on research

Administrative and presentation tasks can be organized so you can focus more of your time on research and revision.

End-to-end

Support across stages

Editing, journal-fit assessment, formatting and submission preparation can be coordinated through one support pathway.

Academic guidance

Subject-aware review

Topic experts and experienced reviewers can help identify clarity, structure and presentation issues before submission.

Important: Journal selection does not guarantee peer review, acceptance, publication, indexing or a specific publication timeline. Authors should always review the current official journal website, scope, peer-review process, publication ethics, fees and submission rules.
