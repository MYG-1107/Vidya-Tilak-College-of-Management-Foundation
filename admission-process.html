Admissions

Admission guidance from 10+2 through postgraduate and research study.

Different institutions use different admission procedures. We help you prepare for the official process at the stage you are applying for.

01 — 10+2

Choose your Bachelor’s route

Identify regular, online or distance undergraduate programmes based on subjects, location, budget and future study plans.

02 — Bachelor’s

Plan for Master’s

Check whether your undergraduate degree, marks, subject background and work experience meet the target programme’s conditions.

03 — Master’s / qualifying degree

Prepare for PhD

Review the institution’s doctoral notice, research area, entrance requirement, proposal expectations and supervisor process.

04 — PhD completed

Explore post-doctoral work

Look for institution-led fellowships, funded projects and research opportunities that match your specialisation.

05 — Documents

Build your checklist

Prepare academic records, identity documents, photographs, category documents and programme-specific certificates as required.

06 — Apply

Use the official admission route

Complete the concerned institution’s application, pay only through official channels and retain your application/fee acknowledgement.

For students

What to tell a counsellor

  • Latest qualification and year of passing
  • Preferred programme and specialisation
  • Regular, online or distance preference
  • City / relocation preference
  • Budget range and desired intake
  • Career or research goal
For institutions

Admissions outreach & collaboration

Colleges and universities can contact Vidya Tilak to discuss a formal student outreach or admissions-counselling arrangement. We do not publish a partnership as confirmed until the institution authorises it in writing.
