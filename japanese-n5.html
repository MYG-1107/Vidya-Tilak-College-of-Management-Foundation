Japanese N5

Japanese N5 — Beginner Japanese Learning Path

Begin with foundational Japanese and build the knowledge needed for N5-oriented learning and practice.

N5 learning areas

Start with the language basics.

The official JLPT describes N5 as the ability to understand some basic Japanese. N5 and N4 are scored using language knowledge/reading and listening components.

Writing systems

Hiragana & Katakana

Learn the two core kana scripts used throughout beginner Japanese study and reading practice.

Vocabulary

Everyday words

Build vocabulary for introductions, time, family, places, food, numbers, routines and common situations.

Grammar & listening

Short practical sentences

Practise simple sentence patterns, particles, basic questions and short listening activities.

Progression

N5 → N4

Once the beginner foundations are stable, learners can progress toward N4-oriented study with broader vocabulary, grammar, reading and listening.
