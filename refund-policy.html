Refund Policy

Refunds depend on the actual payment route.

Because programme fees and admissions are governed by the concerned university or service provider, refund rules should be confirmed before payment.

University programme fees

Where a learner pays a university directly, the university’s published refund and withdrawal policy applies.

Service or facilitation charges

If a separate service charge is ever applicable, the terms should be disclosed before payment and the receipt should identify the payee.

Payment safety

Do not pay to an account or number supplied through an unverified message. Confirm payment instructions with the official institution or authorised channel.

Questions

Contact vidyatilakcollege@gmail.com before making a payment if you need clarification.
