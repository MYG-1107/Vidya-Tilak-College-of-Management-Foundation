WHY CLASS 12?

Learn the basics before your undergraduate journey begins.

This workshop is intentionally focused on Class 12 and Intermediate 2nd Year students. You are approaching an important transition: from school learning into a Bachelor's degree, where you may encounter new subjects, independent projects, research tasks and digital tools.

AI is becoming part of everyday productivity. Becoming familiar with it now can help you use it thoughtfully as you move into engineering, management, medicine, commerce or another field. The goal is not to let AI do the learning for you. It is to help you understand where it can assist, where it can be wrong and how your own judgement remains essential.

01

Engineering & Technology

Explore how AI can support concept learning, coding practice, technical research and project planning.

02

Management

See how AI can help organise ideas, examine case studies, prepare presentations and explore business problems.

03

Medicine & Health Sciences

Understand AI as a learning and information-support tool, while recognising the importance of expert guidance and verified sources.

04

Commerce & Finance

Discover ways to support learning in accounting, economics, data interpretation and business communication.

Responsible use matters: AI-generated content can be incomplete or incorrect. Always verify important information, follow your school or university's academic-integrity rules, protect personal data and use your own understanding.
