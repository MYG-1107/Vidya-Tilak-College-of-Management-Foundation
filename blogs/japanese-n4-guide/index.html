Japanese N4 Guide: What the Level Means and How to Progress from N5

N4 follows N5 in the JLPT five-level structure. The official JLPT describes N4 as the ability to understand basic Japanese. This guide explains a practical transition from beginner study to N4-oriented preparation.

Updated September 30, 2026 · Educational guide by Vidya Tilak College of Management Foundation

N4 compared with N5

N5 is described as understanding some basic Japanese, while N4 represents the ability to understand basic Japanese. The two levels continue to use language knowledge, reading and listening as core assessed areas.

What to strengthen before N4

  • Faster recall of beginner vocabulary
  • More reliable grammar recognition
  • Longer and more varied reading passages
  • Listening to everyday contexts
  • Kanji recognition appropriate to your study plan

A simple N5 → N4 progression

  1. Review your N5 vocabulary and grammar.
  2. Add a steady N4 vocabulary list.
  3. Read short paragraphs instead of isolated sentences.
  4. Listen to everyday conversations repeatedly.
  5. Take practice tests to identify weak areas.

Keep the goal practical

Language exams are only one possible learning goal. You can also use N4-oriented study to develop broader everyday comprehension and confidence.
