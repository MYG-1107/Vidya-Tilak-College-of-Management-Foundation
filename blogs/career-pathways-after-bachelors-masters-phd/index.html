Career Pathways After Bachelor’s, Master’s and PhD

Understand practical next steps after a Bachelor’s, Master’s or PhD: skills, projects, internships, research, teaching, consulting and further study.

In this guideAfter a Bachelor’sAfter a Master’sAfter a PhDBuild evidence, not only credentials

After a Bachelor’s

Common next steps include entry-level employment, internships, professional certifications, a Master’s degree, specialised training or building a portfolio of projects.

After a Master’s

Depending on the field, graduates may progress into specialist roles, management, consulting, teaching, professional qualifications or doctoral research.

After a PhD

Possible directions include post-doctoral research, university teaching, industry research, policy, consulting, innovation, scientific communication and interdisciplinary work. The right route depends heavily on discipline and profile.

Build evidence, not only credentials

Projects, publications, conference activity, teaching experience, internships, portfolios and professional networks can help demonstrate capability alongside formal qualifications.
