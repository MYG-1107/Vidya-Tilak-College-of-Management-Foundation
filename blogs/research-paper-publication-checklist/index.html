Research Paper Publication Checklist for First-Time Authors

A practical pre-submission checklist covering scope fit, manuscript quality, references, formatting, ethics and submission readiness.

Blogs / Research Paper Publication Checklist for First-Time Authors

Before submission, make sure the paper fits the journal’s scope, follows its formatting rules and clearly explains the contribution. Review references, tables, figures, ethics statements and author information before final submission.

Practical guide

Then verify the journal’s current peer-review process, publication fees, indexing claims and submission instructions on the journal’s own website. Publication support can improve readiness but cannot guarantee acceptance.

What to verify

  • Current programme or institution information
  • Eligibility and any programme-specific requirements
  • Duration, complete fee schedule and payment terms
  • Learning mode, assessment and participation expectations
  • Recognition or professional requirements where relevant
Use official sources for final decisions Website information can change. Confirm the current programme document, admission notice, regulator information or official university instructions before making a commitment.
