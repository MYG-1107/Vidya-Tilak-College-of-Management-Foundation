MBBS Abroad Checklist for Indian Students

A practical checklist covering academic records, identity documents, university verification, visa preparation, finance and pre-departure planning for MBBS abroad.

Blogs / MBBS Abroad Checklist for Indian Students

Students considering MBBS abroad should verify both academic and regulatory factors before selecting a university. Start with the current university information and then check the rules that apply to Indian students.

Practical guide

Prepare academic certificates, identity documents and photographs; verify the university and course; understand fees and living costs; check the current NMC/FMGL and NEET requirements; review visa and accommodation requirements; and retain all official communication.

What to verify

  • Current programme or institution information
  • Eligibility and any programme-specific requirements
  • Duration, complete fee schedule and payment terms
  • Learning mode, assessment and participation expectations
  • Recognition or professional requirements where relevant
Use official sources for final decisions Website information can change. Confirm the current programme document, admission notice, regulator information or official university instructions before making a commitment.
