Japanese N5 vs N4: Which Beginner Level Comes First?

Understand the progression between Japanese N5 and N4 and how to build vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening and study habits.

Blogs / Japanese N5 vs N4: Which Beginner Level Comes First?

Japanese N5 is generally treated as the entry point for beginner study, with N4 representing the next step. Build the foundation first: kana, core vocabulary, basic grammar, simple reading and listening.

Practical guide

Progress through consistent practice rather than rushing to a level label. Use short daily sessions, spaced repetition, listening practice and simple written exercises.
