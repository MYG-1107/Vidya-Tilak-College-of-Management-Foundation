Online MBA guide

Online MBA Specialisations in India: How to Compare Areas of Study

Online MBA specialisations can differ significantly by university. The right comparison starts with the learner’s interests, current experience, target roles and the actual curriculum offered by the university.

Updated September 30, 2026 · Educational guide by Vidya Tilak College of Management Foundation

Common specialisation areas

Marketing

Branding, consumer behaviour, digital marketing and market strategy topics may be included.

Finance

Accounting, financial management, analysis and investment-related topics may appear in the curriculum.

Human Resources

Recruitment, performance, organisational behaviour and people management are common themes.

Other areas you may see

Universities may also offer operations, information systems, business analytics, international business, healthcare management, retail or entrepreneurship. Availability is institution-specific.

Questions to ask before choosing

  • What exact subjects are included?
  • Is the specialisation available in the current session?
  • What is the assessment and examination pattern?
  • What is the total fee?
  • How is recognition/entitlement verified?
  • What career or placement support is actually documented?

Do not choose by title alone

Two programmes with the same specialisation name can have different curricula. Read the syllabus and course outcomes before applying.

Important:

Programme, recognition, admissions, fees and exam requirements can change. Verify the current official source before making an academic or exam decision.
