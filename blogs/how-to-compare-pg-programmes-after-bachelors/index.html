How to Compare Postgraduate Programmes After a Bachelor’s Degree

Learn how to compare postgraduate programmes by specialisation, eligibility, duration, fees, learning format and future academic or career use.

Blogs / How to Compare Postgraduate Programmes After a Bachelor’s Degree

A postgraduate course should match both your current qualification and the direction you want to develop. A general MBA, specialist MBA, MCA, M.Com, MA or M.Sc. can serve different purposes, so compare specialisation, curriculum, entry requirements, duration, fee and future academic options.

Practical guide

Review the exact eligibility, subject prerequisites, work-experience expectations, programme structure, assessment model, fee schedule and the institution’s current admission rules before enrolling.

What to verify

  • Current programme or institution information
  • Eligibility and any programme-specific requirements
  • Duration, complete fee schedule and payment terms
  • Learning mode, assessment and participation expectations
  • Recognition or professional requirements where relevant
Use official sources for final decisions Website information can change. Confirm the current programme document, admission notice, regulator information or official university instructions before making a commitment.
