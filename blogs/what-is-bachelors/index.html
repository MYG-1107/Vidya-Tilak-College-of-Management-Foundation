What is a Bachelor’s Degree?

A Bachelor’s degree is an undergraduate qualification normally pursued after completing 10+2 / Intermediate or an equivalent school-leaving qualification. It builds foundational academic knowledge and can lead to employment, professional study or a Master’s degree.

What does “Bachelor’s” mean?

In India, Bachelor’s programmes commonly run for three or four academic years depending on the discipline and institution. Common examples include BA, BBA, B.Com and BCA. Professional and specialised degrees can have different structures and eligibility.

Who should consider a Bachelor’s degree?

  • Students completing 10+2 / Intermediate
  • Learners looking for a first university qualification
  • Students planning to continue to a Master’s degree
  • Working learners who need a flexible pathway where an approved online/ODL option is available
