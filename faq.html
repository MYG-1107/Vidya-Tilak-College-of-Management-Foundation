FAQs

Questions learners ask before choosing Online or Distance Education.

Short answers to common questions about universities, programmes, recognition and counselling.

Is Vidya Tilak a university?

No. Vidya Tilak College of Management Foundation is positioned on this website as an education facilitation and counselling platform. The degree is awarded by the concerned university, subject to its programme and regulatory requirements.

Which universities are featured?

The current website highlights Mangalayatan University, Dr. D. Y. Patil Vidyapeeth, Pimpri, Pune and Mata Tripura Sundari Open University. Additional universities should be added only when their programme information and any required authorisation are documented.

Which programmes can I explore?

The site focuses on undergraduate and postgraduate pathways such as BA, BBA, BCA, B.Com, MBA, MCA, M.Com, MA and M.Sc. Mathematics, subject to university-specific availability.

Are all courses UGC approved?

Do not assume that. Recognition or entitlement can be programme-, mode- and session-specific. Verify the exact current status before admission.

Can working professionals apply?

Many Online and Distance Education programmes are designed to provide flexibility, but eligibility and attendance/examination rules vary. Check the exact university programme information.

Can you tell me the current fees?

Fees can change by programme, session, university and payment plan. Where a fee is shown from a supplied promotional reference, it is labelled as such; always confirm the official fee before payment.

How do I start?

Use the Course Finder or enquiry form, share your qualification and preferred programme, and then review the official university admission route before submitting an application.

Do you provide degree certificates?

No. The concerned university is the degree-awarding institution. Vidya Tilak provides guidance and support around the learner journey.

Online / ODL verification: Recognition can be session- and programme-specific. Confirm the relevant institution and programme on the current UGC DEB information before admission.
