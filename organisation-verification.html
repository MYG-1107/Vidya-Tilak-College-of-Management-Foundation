Awarding body

Confirm which university or provider actually awards the degree, certificate or diploma.

02

Recognition & mode

For regulated or online/ODL study, confirm the institution, programme and study mode for the intended session.

03

Money & dates

Confirm current fee schedule, intake, taxes, examination charges, refund terms and payment destination before paying.
