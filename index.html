About The Foundation

Our Identity

Vidya Tilak College of Management Foundation is a registered Section 8 company (limited by guarantee) incorporated under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India.

Formed with a commitment to educational development and charitable objectives, the foundation works toward skill development, academic guidance, and leadership formation.

Key Objectives

  • Promoting educational advancement and management training.
  • Facilitating skill building and empowerment programs.
  • Implementing government welfare and grant-in-aid schemes.
