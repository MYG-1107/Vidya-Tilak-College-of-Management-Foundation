Recognition & Compliance

Recognition should be checked programme by programme.

University status alone does not answer every programme-level question. Learners should verify the applicable recognition or entitlement for the exact university, programme, mode and academic session.

University

University status

Check the university’s current legal and regulatory status from official sources.

Programme

Programme availability

Confirm that the exact programme is offered in the mode and session you intend to join.

Mode

Online vs ODL

Do not assume that a university’s recognition for one mode automatically answers the status of another programme or mode.

Session

Academic session

Verify current entitlement/recognition and admission instructions for the session you are entering.

Verification checklist

  1. University name and type
  2. Relevant UGC status
  3. UGC-DEB status / entitlement where applicable
  4. Exact programme and mode
  5. Academic session
  6. Official university programme page
  7. Official admission / payment route
Responsible communication: Vidya Tilak should not publish blanket claims such as “all courses are UGC approved.” Recognition and regulatory status are programme-, mode- and session-specific. Check UGC-DEB ↗
