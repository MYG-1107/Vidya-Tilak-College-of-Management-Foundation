Japanese Language Learning — N5 & N4 Beginner Pathways

Build Japanese foundations with a structured progression from N5-oriented beginner study to N4-oriented elementary study.

JLPT N5

Understand some basic Japanese

The JLPT describes N5 as the ability to understand some basic Japanese. Learning focuses on foundational vocabulary, grammar, reading and listening.

N5 learning page →
JLPT N4

Understand basic Japanese

The JLPT describes N4 as the ability to understand basic Japanese. It builds beyond beginner foundations with wider vocabulary, grammar, reading and listening.

Skill areas

Build the four practical foundations.

  • Hiragana and katakana
  • Core vocabulary
  • Grammar patterns and sentence building
  • Reading practice
  • Listening practice
  • Everyday communication
JLPT note:

N5 and N4 are two of the five JLPT levels. The official test is administered by the Japan Foundation and Japan Educational Exchanges and Services through the JLPT system.
