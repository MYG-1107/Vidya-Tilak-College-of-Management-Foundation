German language learning

German Language Learning — Beginner A1 & A2 Pathway

Learn German step by step with practical communication, vocabulary and CEFR-aligned beginner objectives.

A1

German A1 — first beginner level

A1 focuses on familiar everyday expressions, very basic phrases, introductions and simple communication. It is the first level in the six-level CEFR scale used by the Goethe-Institut.

A2

German A2 — elementary communication

A2 expands everyday communication into routine situations and familiar topics such as people, work, shopping and the immediate environment.

Learning focus

What learners can practise

  • Everyday vocabulary and useful phrases
  • Pronunciation and listening
  • Basic reading and writing
  • Introducing yourself and asking questions
  • Routine conversations and practical situations
Certification note

The Goethe-Institut offers German examinations from A1 to C2. Vidya Tilak language learning support is separate from official exam administration and certification.
