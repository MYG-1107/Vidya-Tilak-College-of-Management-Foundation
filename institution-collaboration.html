How the market works

Study the model before proposing a partnership.

Current education websites show different approaches: some organisations publicly describe authorised admission-partner arrangements, while some universities explicitly state that they do not authorise agents or admission consultants. Vidya Tilak will confirm the institution’s own policy before describing any relationship as authorised.

Example • self-described

SNIT partner model

Sant Namdev Institute of Technology publicly describes itself as an authorised admission partner for Guru Kashi University, Glocal University and Arni University. This is a third-party self-description, not a Vidya Tilak relationship.

See example ↗
Example • self-described

Education network model

Future Edu Group publicly describes authorised-admission-partner relationships with selected universities. This illustrates the importance of written terms, approved programmes and institution-specific brand usage.

See example ↗
Important counter-example

Some universities prohibit agents

MAHE’s current public advisory states that it has not authorised agents, middlemen or admission consultants for admissions. A prospective partner must therefore check each institution’s current policy first.

See official advisory ↗
Working principle for Vidya TilakUse a written agreement or formal institutional communication that defines the programmes, intake/session, responsibilities, student-data handling, commercial terms, brand use and the exact admission role permitted.
