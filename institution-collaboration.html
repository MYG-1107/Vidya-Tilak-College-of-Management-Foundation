How an enquiry is reviewed

A documented scope before any commitment.

1

Brief

Share your organisation, audience, topic, location, format and intended outcome.

2

Review

We review fit, capacity, permissions, timing and the information needed to scope the work.

3

Clarify

Discuss responsibilities, deliverables, commercial terms, data handling and any approvals required.

4

Confirm

Work begins only after the scope and terms are agreed in writing by the relevant parties.

Transparency: A submitted enquiry does not create a partnership, authorisation or service agreement. Vidya Tilak will not describe an institution as a partner or an admission arrangement as authorised without current written confirmation.
What to include

Help us understand the requirement.

A useful first brief describes the intended audience, subject, delivery format, location, timeframe, expected output and any institutional policies or approvals that apply. Do not send confidential student records or sensitive personal data at this stage.
