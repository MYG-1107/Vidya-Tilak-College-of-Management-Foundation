Privacy

How enquiry information is handled.

We use submitted contact information to respond to education enquiries and provide requested guidance.

Information you may submit

Name, mobile number, email, qualification, year of passing, programme interest, preferred university, location and message.

Why we use it

To respond to enquiries, provide programme information, coordinate counselling and follow up on an education request.

Sharing

Where necessary to respond to a programme enquiry, information may be used to coordinate with the relevant university or service provider. Do not submit sensitive information that is not required for your enquiry.

Contact

For privacy questions, email enquiry@vidyatilakcollege.org.
