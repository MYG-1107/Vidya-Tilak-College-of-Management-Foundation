Career pathways

Build a career plan around every stage of higher education.

A degree, master’s qualification or PhD can open different directions. Use this guide to understand the common next steps and build a profile around skills, evidence of work, professional networks, research and continued learning.

After 10+2 / Bachelor’s

Undergraduate → Career Launch

Choose a bachelor’s route that matches your interests and target roles. Alongside the qualification, develop communication, digital literacy, problem-solving and domain skills.

  • Build projects and a portfolio
  • Seek internships or practical exposure
  • Develop workplace communication
  • Prepare for entry-level roles or higher study
After Bachelor’s

Master’s → Career Growth

A postgraduate qualification can support specialist, managerial, analytical or technical career directions depending on the discipline and experience.

  • Choose a specialisation aligned to your target role
  • Build case studies, projects and measurable outcomes
  • Strengthen leadership and industry skills
  • Consider professional roles, entrepreneurship or research
After Master’s / PhD

Doctoral → Research & Knowledge Careers

Doctoral study can lead into academic, research, innovation, consulting, policy and specialist professional pathways. Your research output and professional network become important career assets.

  • Publish and present research responsibly
  • Build teaching or research experience
  • Explore academic, industry R&D and consulting roles
  • Consider post-doctoral and specialist research opportunities
One journey, multiple routes

Qualification → Skills → Evidence → Opportunity → Next step.

There is no single career outcome for a qualification. Your subject, experience, skills, projects, interests and the requirements of employers or research institutions all influence the next step. Vidya Tilak’s role is to help you understand the options and prepare questions before you choose.
