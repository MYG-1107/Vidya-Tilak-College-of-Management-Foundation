INTERNATIONAL MEDICAL EDUCATION

MBBS Abroad: from university search to pre-departure readiness.

Vidya Tilak provides counselling and application support for students exploring medical education abroad. The service covers destination discovery, university comparison, document readiness, application guidance, visa planning, accommodation information, finance guidance and pre-departure preparation.

Enquire about MBBS AbroadView document guide
Destinations to explore

Country options shown in the current public medical-education catalogue.

Students may explore destinations such as Barbados, Kyrgyzstan, Georgia, Tajikistan, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. Availability, university choice, fees and admission criteria vary; verify every institution and current rule before proceeding.

Barbados

Explore medical education options while checking university credentials, programme structure, total costs and Indian regulatory requirements.

Kyrgyzstan

Compare institutions, tuition, living arrangements and the current Indian student compliance requirements.

Georgia

Understand admission documents, programme format, living costs and current recognition requirements before enrollment.

Tajikistan

Review university-specific admission rules, total cost and student support arrangements.

Kazakhstan

Compare medical universities, admission routes and support services on an institution-by-institution basis.

Uzbekistan

Check the exact university, curriculum, eligibility and current professional rules before applying.

Medical education verification: No country or university should be treated as automatically suitable. Students should independently verify current NMC/FMGL rules, NEET eligibility, university status, admission requirements, internship/licensing conditions, fees and immigration requirements before making a financial commitment.
