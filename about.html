About the foundation

Creating platforms for better education and research.

Vidya Tilak College of Management Foundation is focused on academic development, multidisciplinary research, knowledge exchange and institutional collaboration.

Our story

Built to connect people, knowledge and institutions.

The foundation was incorporated on 28 November 2022 as a Section 8 Company Limited by Guarantee under the Companies Act, 2013.

Its purpose is to provide a structured platform for academicians, researchers, institutions and professionals to collaborate around contemporary educational and research priorities.

Rather than treating education as a standalone activity, our approach brings learning, research, events and institutional development into one connected ecosystem.

Ignited Passion for Research

We encourage multidisciplinary inquiry and knowledge exchange that can contribute to meaningful local and global conversations.
