About Vidya Tilak

Connecting learners with the right university, programme and opportunity.

Vidya Tilak College of Management Foundation is being positioned as a student-centric education facilitation and counselling platform for Online, Distance and flexible higher education.

Our role

A guidance platform — not a degree-awarding university

Our role is to make programme discovery easier: explain options, help learners compare them, support documentation and guide applicants toward the concerned university’s official admission process.

Discover

Programme information

Understand course level, study mode, duration and other published programme details.

Decide

Programme comparison

Put options side by side so important differences are easier to see.

Proceed

Application guidance

Get help understanding the next steps and documentation required by the university.

Our principles

Friendly guidance, transparent information and responsible communication

01

Clarity first

We aim to present eligibility, mode and programme information in plain language.

02

Choice without pressure

Learners should understand their options before deciding where and how to study.

03

Support throughout

From initial enquiry to application guidance, we focus on useful next steps.

04

Verify before admission

Recognition, entitlement, fees and admission rules can change by university, programme, mode and session.

Online / ODL verification: Recognition can be session- and programme-specific. Confirm the relevant institution and programme on the current UGC DEB information before admission.
