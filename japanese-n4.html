Japanese N4 — Elementary Japanese Learning Path

Continue from beginner foundations toward N4-oriented Japanese through broader vocabulary, grammar, reading and listening practice.

N4 learning areas

Move beyond the first beginner stage.

The official JLPT describes N4 as the ability to understand basic Japanese. Learners work with more language than at N5 while continuing to develop the same core skills.

Language knowledge

Vocabulary & grammar expansion

Develop broader vocabulary and more sentence patterns so you can understand and produce common expressions with greater confidence.

Reception skills

Reading & listening

Practise short texts and everyday listening situations, with attention to meaning, context and familiar communication patterns.

A practical bridge toward higher Japanese study.

N4-oriented learning can serve as a structured step after beginner study and before intermediate JLPT levels. Consistent reading, listening and vocabulary revision are central to progress.
